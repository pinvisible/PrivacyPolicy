Our story

Medicine with
a softer touch.

KIMS began with a simple belief: that the most advanced medicine should never feel cold. Our transplant programme was founded by surgeons who had trained at the world's best centres — and who returned home convinced that excellent care and warm care could be the same thing.

Today, we are an integrated multi-organ transplant institute. Our nephrologists, cardiologists, pulmonologists and intensivists work as one team. Our coordinators stay with families from the first phone call through years of follow-up. The technology is world-class. The care is personal.
