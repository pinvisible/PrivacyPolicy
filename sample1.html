About KIMS
Built around the patient, backed by science

Established with a vision to make life-saving transplant care accessible, KIMS Hospital has grown into one of India's most trusted multi-organ transplant centres. Our integrated approach brings together transplant surgeons, nephrologists, cardiologists, pulmonologists and intensivists under one roof.

From your first consultation through years of follow-up, our coordinators stay with you every step of the way.
