Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about safety, Nukh, and calling.

Why is there a mandatory ₹149 / $5 one-time activation fee?

Unlike commercial platforms that allow millions of dormant or fake accounts, our ₹149 fee covers official DigiLocker / Passport verification and ensures that 100% of candidates on our platform are genuine, active, and serious about marriage.

How does the Nukh Exogamy filter work?

During profile setup, you provide your Paternal Nukh and Maternal Nukh (Mosal). When browsing, our automated filter automatically excludes proposals that share your direct paternal lineage, strictly adhering to traditional Sindhi custom.

Can anyone call me on audio or video randomly?

Never. In-app audio and video calling is strictly protected by a Mutual Consent Framework. You can only be called by an accepted connection during your pre-set calling hours. Your mobile number is never exposed.

What is the difference between Candidate Mode and Parent Mode?

Candidates can view lifestyle attributes, personal career credentials, and chat directly. Parents can switch with one tap to focus on family background, parental occupations, joint family values, and 36-Guna Kundli charts.
