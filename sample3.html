About Us
Welcome to KIMS Transplant Institute

One of India's Most Trusted Transplant Centres

KIMS Transplant Institute is dedicated to providing multi-disciplinary integrated healthcare services, with a focus on tertiary and quaternary care at affordable cost. Our centre brings together transplant surgeons, nephrologists, cardiologists, pulmonologists and intensivists under one roof, supported by world-class infrastructure.

From your first consultation through years of post-operative follow-up, our coordinators stay with you and your family every step of the way — combining the technical excellence of leading global centres with the personal care that defines us.
